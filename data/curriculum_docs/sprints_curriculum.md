# Sprints AI & Software Engineering Curriculum Guide

## Track 1: Agentic AI & LLM Engineering
- **Module AI-101: Python Foundations & Data Structures**
  - Prerequisites: None.
  - Topics: Functions, recursion, error handling, OOP, and clean modular scripting.
  - Passing Requirement: Minimum score of 70% on live coding assessment.

- **Module AI-102: Prompt Engineering & Structured Outputs**
  - Prerequisites: Module AI-101.
  - Topics: System prompts, Pydantic schema validation, JSON mode, and deterministic temperature control.

- **Module AI-103: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) & Vector Databases**
  - Prerequisites: Module AI-102.
  - Topics: Document chunking (500 tokens, 50 overlap), embeddings, ChromaDB persistence, semantic search, and self-reflective query rewriting.

- **Module AI-104: Multi-Agent Orchestration with LangGraph**
  - Prerequisites: Module AI-103.
  - Topics: TypedDict state graphs, conditional routing, MemorySaver checkpointing, Evaluator-Optimizer loops, and Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) interrupts.

## Track 2: Backend & Cloud Engineering
- **Module BE-201: RESTful APIs with FastAPI & SQL**
  - Prerequisites: Module AI-101.
  - Topics: Relational schema design, SQLite3/PostgreSQL queries, Pydantic validation, and async endpoints.

- **Module BE-202: Production Deployment & System Design**
  - Prerequisites: Module BE-201.
  - Topics: Microservices, caching, latency optimization (<1000ms SLA), and CI/CD pipelines.

## Career Placement & Hiring Partner Requirements

### Tier-1 Strategic Hiring Partners (Fawry, InstaPay, Vodafone Intelligent Solutions)
- **Required Modules:** Candidates targeting **Fawry** or **InstaPay** must complete **AI-101, AI-102, AI-103, AI-104**, and **BE-201** with an assessment average of **80% or higher**.
- **Escalation Policy (Mandatory Human Review):** Any placement recommendation, mock interview scheduling, or salary negotiation guidance involving a **Tier-1 Hiring Partner** (specifically **Fawry**, **InstaPay**, or **Vodafone**) requires explicit **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Career Coach approval** before finalizing advice to the learner.
- **Compensation Benchmarks (Egypt Market):**
  - Junior AI / Backend Engineer (0–1 yr): 18,000 – 25,000 EGP/month.
  - Mid-Level AI / Agentic Systems Engineer (1–3 yrs): 28,000 – 45,000 EGP/month.

### Tier-2 Hiring Partners (Startup & Regional Tech Network)
- **Required Modules:** Completion of **AI-101, AI-102, and AI-103** with a minimum score of **70%**.
- **Escalation Policy:** Automated placement advice is permitted without human coach approval.
